How to interpret these results

TWO-BOOK FAIR Both FD and DK have paired Over/Under reference prices (exact or tightly interpolated). No-vig probabilities are averaged, and an estimated ROI is calculated. This is a market estimate, not a guaranteed edge.

PRICE ONLY FD/DK Over prices are available but fair probability cannot be established. A favorable price gap is a shopping opportunity; no EV or fair probability is invented. A 2-point price gap does not imply +2% expected value.

NO REFERENCE Missing comparable prices or an unsupported threshold. Not ranked as a value opportunity.

Line-to-line interpolation is allowed only between two paired reference thresholds from the same sportsbook, separated by at most 25 yards, with monotonic probabilities. No extrapolation or arbitrary probability discounts. For one-sided alternate Overs, the displayed raw implied probabilities include bookmaker margin. No bets are placed.
